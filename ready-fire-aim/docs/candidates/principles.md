- id: p01
  title: 创业第一法则：销售至上
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Chapter 4 — The Supremacy of Selling
  source_quote: |
    "Without sales, it is very hard to sustain an ongoing business. Consider this to be Rule Number One of Entrepreneurship."
  summary: |

  tags: [sales, priority, stage-one, cash-flow]

- id: p10
  title: 前端折扣引流 + 后端品质盈利
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Chapter 5 — Your Optimum Selling Strategy
  source_quote: |
    "The ideal pricing mix for me is one lead product that is promoted at a heavy discount and all other products sold by emphasizing quality."
  summary: |
    理想定价组合是：用一款大幅折扣的前端产品吸引新客户，再用强调品质的后端产品获取利润。
  tags: [pricing, front-end, back-end, customer-acquisition]

- id: p11
  title: 可承受获客成本（AAC）公式
  type: rule
  source_chapter: Chapter 5 — Your Optimum Selling Strategy
  source_quote: |
    "Figure out how much a customer will spend over a lifetime. Deduct costs and desired profit. The remainder is your allowable acquisition cost."
  summary: |
    计算可承受获客成本：客户终身消费 − 商品成本 − 分摊 overhead − 期望利润 = 可为获取首次客户承受的最大亏损。
  tags: [aac, customer-lifetime-value, pricing, metrics]

- id: ce01
  title: 把琐事放在销售之前
  type: counter-example
  source_chapter: 'Chapter 4: The Supremacy of Selling'
  source_quote: '"I''ve seen a lot more businesses go broke because they didn''t have
    enough sales than I''ve seen go under from lack of computers. Why don''t you work
    on first things first?"'
  failure_mode: 创业初期把时间和资金花在电脑、办公室、宣传册等非销售事务上，却不去直接获取客户和订单。
  mechanism: 婴儿期企业的生命线是销售收入和现金流；没有销售，基础设施再完善也毫无意义，现金会在准备阶段耗尽。
  warning_signs:
  - 开业先装修办公室、买设备、做名片
  - 以'还没准备好'为由推迟销售
  - 每天忙碌却没有新增订单
  bound_to:
  - 婴儿期企业销售优先任务清单
  - 新商业想法的快速市场验证（低成本测试销售主张）

  type: counter-example
  source_chapter: 'Chapter 6: Mastering the Copy Side of Selling'

  - 获客成本高于客户首单贡献

  - 后端转化率极低

  type: counter-example
  source_chapter: 'Chapter 12: Getting Ready'
  source_quote: '"The key to making a Plan B is to set stop-loss points at the outset—measurable
